# Motion

Motion in a tool or dashboard explains state. It does not introduce the window. Durations for ordinary chrome live in the craft floor (150–250ms, interruptible, state only). Gesture physics stay in the apple-design skill. Do not copy them into a dashboard.

## When

A selection, expand, split, or value change is hard to follow, or the window plays a load sequence, a pulse, or a bounce.

## Procedure

1. Write the motion thesis in one line: the state change this motion explains. If there is no state change, do not add motion.
2. One material idea. Opacity or a short transform for selection and disclosure. Do not blur the data layer to make a chart feel deep. Glass rules stay in [canon/materials.md](canon/materials.md).
3. The change can be grabbed or reversed. Exit is at least as fast as enter. No bounce, no elastic, no staggered page load.
4. Reduced motion keeps the state legible (color, selection, text) and drops the travel.

## Refuses

- A pulsing status dot as life.
- Load choreography before the sovereign surface is usable.
- Animating width, height, or layout in a way that shifts the reading position on every tick.
- A focal marketing entrance on a dashboard.

## Verify

Repeat the action twice. The second time is as clear as the first, and focus never waits on the animation. Hand off to `polish`.
